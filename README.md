# Logistics-operations-dashboard
End-to-end Power BI logistics analytics project analyzing 85,000+ delivery trips, fleet performance, revenue, and operational KPIs.
**What is this?**
A Power BI dashboard analyzing over 85,000 delivery trips across 14 data tables to uncover operational inefficiencies in a logistics company.
**Problem Statement**: How can a logistics company reduce trip delays, optimize route performance, monitor driver efficiency, and improve overall delivery operations using data?
Tools Used: Power BI, DAX (calculated measures & columns), Power Query (data transformation), Excel (source data), Data Modeling (14-table schema with defined relationships).

**Key Insights**:
1. A significant volume of trips experienced delays, with certain routes and time periods showing consistently higher failure rates
2. Driver performance varied widely, a small group of drivers accounted for a disproportionate share of incomplete or delayed trips
3. Fuel consumption patterns revealed inefficiencies tied to specific vehicle types and route distances
4. Peak operational hours and days showed demand-supply mismatches, suggesting scheduling gaps
5. Trip revenue vs. cost analysis highlighted routes that were high in volume but low in profitability
   
**Skills Demonstrated**: Data modeling (14-table relational schema), DAX measures and KPIs, Power Query data cleaning and transformation, multi-page dashboard design, business storytelling through visualization, color-themed reporting (Midnight Purple & Silver Lavender)
